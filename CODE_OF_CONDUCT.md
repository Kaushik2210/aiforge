# Code of Conduct

We want AIForge to be a welcoming place for learners and contributors of every
background and experience level.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Welcome beginners; questions are never "too basic".
- Credit others' work and cite sources.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or insults.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Spam, or content that deliberately misleads learners.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions that break these rules and
may restrict participation for repeat or serious violations. Report concerns by
opening a private security advisory on the repository or contacting the
maintainer listed in the repository profile.

This policy is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
